# Name research: shortlist and evidence (2026-11-16)

**Status: proposal only.** The live site keeps the name DealPilot until the owner approves a change. No domain was bought or reserved. The brand name is centralised in code so it can be swapped in one place.

## What the name must carry
The product's real difference is honesty: a final price, no invented discounts, and checked links. The name should say "the bottom line" in a way an Israeli shopper hears instantly. It should also work in English, be short, and stay available.

## Method
- **Domain availability:** checked live with the Vercel domain registry tool. A read-only lookup; nothing was reserved.
- **Conflicts:** web search, Hebrew and English.
- **Not checked:** Israeli `.co.il` domains (not covered by the tool) and the Israeli trademark registry. Both are marked NOT CHECKED, and the owner should check them before any purchase.

## Recommendation
**תכל'ס (Tachles)** as the working name.
- **Tagline:** "המחיר בשורה התחתונה" ("the price, bottom line").
- **Domain to consider first:** `tachles.shop`, after the owner checks `tachles.co.il` and the trademark register.
- **Why:**
  - It is the product's promise in one word.
  - It is instantly Israeli, with no translation needed.
  - It is short and memorable.
  - Domains are available.
  - No category conflict was found. DealPilot, by contrast, has a direct one.

## The symbol (works with any name)
A price tag whose body is underlined by a heavy bar: the "bottom line". The tag hole is the dot of a check. It reads as tag + bottom line + verified. It is not a shopping cart. Files: `public/brand/`.
