# Brand logo files (`public/brands/`)

**Rule:** a logo is shown only to identify the brand of a listed product.
- It does not imply affiliation. The footer says the site is independent and not affiliated with AliExpress or the brands shown.
- Logos are used **only** from sources whose copyright licence allows reuse.
- An official mark is never drawn or imitated. A brand without a free file keeps the typographic monogram (first letter + name).

## From official brand websites (owner decision, 2026-11-18)
The owner chose to use each brand's logo as published on its own official website, to identify that brand's products. Copyright and trademark stay with the brand. The footer states the site is independent and not affiliated with the brands. Files were scanned for scripts and handlers; none found.

Edits made to the files:
- Empty margins were trimmed.
- PNGs were resized to at most 160 px high.
- SVG viewBoxes were cropped to the drawing.
- Dreame and Reolink publish a white header logo, so the fill was set to near-black (#151515) for light backgrounds. The shape is unchanged.
